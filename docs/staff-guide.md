# Faida: staff guide

How to use the till. Training takes 15–30 minutes. Go through each part on the screen with the trainer.

## Log in and lock

- Type your **PIN** on the keypad. Your tables come up.
- Press **LOCK** whenever you walk away. The screen also locks by itself if nobody touches it.

## Take an order

1. Tap a **free table**. For a customer without a table, use **New tab** and type their name. For a quick sale at the bar, use **Quick sale**.
2. Tap a **category**, then an item. Some items ask a question first:
   - **Size**, for example single or double tot.
   - **Choices**, for example the mixer, the side, or how the steak is cooked. A choice marked *required* must be picked. Some choices add to the price.
3. Tap **SEND**. Food prints in the kitchen and drinks print at the bar. Nothing is made without a slip.
4. The same table orders the same again? Tap **Same again** on that round.

Things to know:

- A **grey tile marked SOLD OUT** can't be ordered tonight. The supervisor marks items sold out and back.
- During **happy hour** the screen shows a banner, the offer prices show with the old price crossed out, and the bill shows the offer.
- A **combo** (for example chicken & chips + soda) is one line on the bill. Its parts go to the kitchen and the bar by themselves.
- A table with an **orange printer sign** has a slip that didn't print. Tell the supervisor.

## Fix a mistake

Tap the item on the bill:

- **Remove it**: say whether it was already prepared or not. A waiter needs the supervisor's PIN.
- **Give it free**: choose the reason (on the house, staff drink, owner's guest, complaint, promotion). This also needs the supervisor's PIN. The item stays on the bill at 0, and the owner sees it in the closing.

## Get paid

1. Tap **BILL** to print the bill for the customer. Tap **PAY** when they are ready.
2. Choose how they pay:
   - **Cash**: type what they gave you. The screen shows the **change**. Press **TAKE CASH** and the drawer opens.
   - **M-Pesa**: check the customer's message, type the **last 4 characters** of the code, and press **RECORD M-PESA**. If the screen says the code was already used, stop and call the supervisor.
   - **Card**: when the card machine says *Approved*, type the **last 4 characters** from the card slip and press **RECORD CARD**.
   - **Credit**: only for approved customers. Press **REQUEST CREDIT**.
3. A customer paying part cash and part M-Pesa? Record one payment, then the next, until the balance is 0.
4. **PAID** shows and the receipt prints. You can see the receipt on the screen.

## End your shift

1. Make sure all your bills are paid, handed over, or marked **Unpaid** if the customer left without paying.
2. Press **END MY SHIFT**. Count your cash and type it in.
3. The supervisor checks your M-Pesa and card payments and receives the cash with their PIN.
4. The screen shows if you are **balanced**, short or over. A shortage is recorded against your name.

## Supervisor and owner

- **Approvals**: when a waiter needs approval, type your PIN on their screen. Never tell them your PIN.
- **Sold out**: on the order screen, tap **SOLD OUT**, then tap the items to mark them. Tap them again when they are back.
- **Owner area**: shift hand-overs, credit, the cash box, stock, finished bills, printers, the daily closing and reports. The owner also sees profit and **Setup**: menu and prices, pictures, choices and happy hour, tables, staff and PINs, and settings.
- **Daily closing**: after the last shift ends, go to Owner area > Daily closing and press **CLOSE DAY**. The report is kept, sent to the owner's phone, and emailed if emails are set. The **Excel** button saves it for the accountant, and **Print / PDF** prints it or saves it as a PDF.
