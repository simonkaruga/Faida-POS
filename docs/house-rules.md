# House rules for the till

These rules apply to everyone who sells, from the first day. Read them, ask about anything that isn't clear, and sign at the bottom.

1. **Your PIN is yours.** Never tell anyone your PIN, and never use someone else's. Everything done under your PIN is counted as yours.
2. **Lock the screen when you walk away.** Press LOCK. If you forget, the screen locks by itself after a short time.
3. **Nothing leaves the bar or kitchen without a slip.** Every drink and every plate is ordered on the screen and SENT first. No slip, no drink.
4. **You are responsible for your bills.** The money for every bill you open must come back to you, in cash, by M-Pesa, by card, or as approved credit.
5. **M-Pesa: check the message, then type the last 4 characters.** Check the customer's M-Pesa message before you enter the code. If the screen says the code was already used, stop and call the supervisor.
6. **Card: type the last 4 characters of the card slip.** Do this only once the card machine has said "Approved".
7. **Removing an item, a discount, a free item or a reprint needs a supervisor's or the owner's PIN.** Never ask anyone for their PIN to do it yourself.
8. **Free drinks are recorded.** Staff drinks, the owner's guests and complaints are entered as "Give free" with the reason. They are never left off the bill.
9. **Credit only for approved customers, within their limit.** Anything else is your responsibility.
10. **End your shift on the screen and count your cash in front of the supervisor.** Any shortage is recorded against your name.
11. **A bill that isn't paid is yours.** Mark it "unpaid" before you end your shift, so the owner knows.
12. **Report problems straight away.** Tell the supervisor at once if a printer isn't printing, the drawer won't open, or a wrong item was sent.
